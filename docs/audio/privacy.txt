Audio Normalisation
Audio normalization is a process that adjusts the volume of an audio track to achieve consistent sound levels. This ensures a balanced listening experience without altering the original dynamics or tonal characteristics. Audio normalization adjusts the amplitude of an audio signal to a target level, correcting for volume inconsistencies within a single track or across multiple recordings. It does not change dynamic range, EQ, or timing—every sample is multiplied by the same factor. Normalization involves analysing the audio to identify peaks and troughs and applying a linear gain adjustment to achieve the target level.
