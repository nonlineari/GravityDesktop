GRAVITY CHANNELS

postProcessGravityChannels. Checked gravity on a finished recording, and on a finished download that is not a GIF. Discrete packets for Noise transport. mix.wav is the hear-back.

FILE

First audio stream. ffprobe channels. No audio: stop, status “gravity: no audio to split”.

→

NORM

If the box is peak or loudness, one programme filter. off leaves this stage empty.

→

PAN

Identity pan to the layout name. c0=c0 … so channels are labeled, not remixed.

→

SPLIT

channelsplit writes one pcm_s24le 48 kHz WAV per label, at most 8.

→

MIX

amerge of those WAVs, aformat to the same layout, mix.wav beside them.
